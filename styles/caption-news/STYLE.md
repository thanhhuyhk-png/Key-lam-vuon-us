# Garden Caption News (US Edition) — Style Prompt

> **Format Overview**: High-retention, sound-off social video designed specifically for **American home gardeners aged 35–60+** (core mobile audience 60–80). Real footage of plants, soil, macro pests, and authentic University Extension research bulletins serve as the visual ground. Giant, crystal-clear **English captions** are the lead actor, delivered in readable chunks with high-contrast highlight blocks on key numbers, plant names, and chemical mechanisms.
> **Brand Voice**: **Lazy Grandma Wisdom meets Science-Backed Proof** (authoritative educator, reassuring, anti-alarmist, practical, low effort, high yield).
> **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 for YouTube desktop/TV feeds; easily adapted to 9:16 for YouTube Shorts.

---

## 1. Core Essence (Adapted for US Gardening Audience)

1. **Sound-Off First, Mobile-Optimized**: Over 70% of viewers watch muted or while walking their garden. Every beat must be 100% understandable from footage and bold typography alone. The voiceover adds warmth and pacing, not essential data.
2. **English-Only, High-Legibility Typography**:
   - Zero Chinese/bilingual text. Pure US English.
   - Designed for older eyes (35–60+, core 60–80): Ultra-legible sans-serif/slab fonts, minimum size 76–88px on 1080p canvas, high-contrast drop shadows.
   - Chunked delivery: Sentences arrive in 2–3 natural spoken chunks with a subtle scale pop (`1.2 → 1.0`), holding on screen for at least **2.6 to 3.2 seconds** to prevent reading fatigue.
3. **Science-Backed Proof (Tier A Sources Only)**:
   - Every claim, dosage, or warning must carry its source tag:
     - **Land-Grant University Extensions**: `Penn State Extension`, `Univ. of Florida IFAS`, `Texas A&M AgriLife`, `Cornell Cooperative Extension`, `UC Davis / UC ANR`, `Clemson Extension`.
     - **Federal Agencies**: `USDA-ARS`, `USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map`, `EPA`.
     - **Pet & Child Safety**: `ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center (APCC)`.
   - Never cite unverified blogs, Reddit anecdotes, or TikTok gardening myths as authority.
4. **The Hook on Frame 4 (<1.0s)**:
   - Starts immediately with an urgent seasonal warning, a surprising cost/ratio, or a counter-intuitive garden truth (e.g., *"Stop throwing away dead tomato vines in October"* or *"This \$2 kitchen staple actually kills root rot"*).
   - The primary metric/number rolls smoothly to its value in the first 0.4 seconds.
5. **The Narrative Arc ("Myth → University Truth → Lazy Grandma Fix")**:
   - **Beat 1 (0–6s)**: The common habit or expensive garden mistake (bad number or widespread myth).
   - **Beat 2 (6–18s)**: The Turn / Freeze — "Wait, does this actually work?". Silence or drop in background music.
   - **Beat 3 (18–35s)**: University Extension study & biological mechanism (the science revealed on a clean data/document card).
   - **Beat 4 (35–55s)**: The 10-second zero-dollar practical fix (Lazy Grandma method).

## 2. Frame and Layout (1920×1080 Full HD)

| Zone | Position | Visual Spec & Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Footage Plate** | Full Bleed (1920×1080) | Macro garden shots, pest damage, root inspections, or Extension bulletins. Never empty or jittery. |
| **Caption Card** | Left-aligned at `x: 120`, baseline `y: 960` | Bold single-line English text, 80–88px. Chunks slam sequentially. One key phrase highlighted in a colored pill block. |
| **Channel Bug** | Top-left (`x: 96, y: 52`, 44px height) | `GARDEN US` in bold black on Golden Pollen block (`#F9C846`) + topic label in cream small caps. |
| **Source Tag** | Under bug (`y: 108`, 36px height) | White pill `PROOF` + dark grey pill (`Penn State Extension · Study #2024`, `USDA Hardiness Zone 7b`). Slides in over 0.25s. |
| **Footage Credit** | Top-right (`y: 52`), 18px on 50% dark pill | `Footage: USDA-ARS / CC BY 2.0`. |
| **Lower Third** | `x: 120, y: 760`, 84px height | Botanical name (Italics), Plant Hardiness Zones (e.g. `Zones 4–9`), or soil pH indicator. |

* **Gradients**: Bottom dark gradient (opacity 0% at `y: 560` → 85% at `y: 1080`) ensures white captions are 100% readable over bright green foliage or light sky. Top dark gradient (50% → 0% over 180px) protects channel bug and source tag.

## 4. Typography & Caption Card Engine

- **Primary Caption Font**: `Inter`, `Montserrat`, `Oswald`, or `Segoe UI` (weight 800/900).
  - Main text size: `84px` (emphasis/verdict lines `100px`).
  - Drop shadow: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`, blur `16px`, offset-y `4px`.
- **Chunk Slam Motion**:
  - Sentences are marked with `|` in timeline: e.g. `Stop throwing away|your fall leaves|{save $180}`.
  - Chunks slam at `0.14s` intervals: scale `1.25 → 1.0` in `0.16s` with gentle back-ease (overshoot `1.3`).
- **Keyword Highlight Block**:
  - Word enclosed in `{...}`: colored rectangular pill wipes left-to-right behind the text in `0.14s`.
  - Highlighted text turns dark ink (`#121612`) on yellow/coral/green block.
- **Reading Speed Rule for 35–60+**:
  - Maximum 6–8 words per caption card.
  - Duration per card: Spoken length + `0.8s` padding (minimum `2.5s`). Never flash text off-screen before a senior viewer can finish reading.

## 5. Specialized Garden Data Graphics

1. **Extension Study Bulletin Card**:
   - Clean research PDF page positioned at a 2° natural angle on a potting bench slate texture.
   - University seal/header visible at top (e.g. *Penn State Agricultural Extension*).
   - Fluorescent Yellow or Sprout Green highlighter marker wipes across the crucial conclusion sentence (`0.4s`).
   - The key quantified number (e.g. `+43% Root Growth`) pops out as a bold card beside the paper.
2. **Soil pH / Nutrient Slider**:
   - Horizontal bar showing soil pH scale from 4.0 (Acidic) to 8.5 (Alkaline).
   - Optimal zone for the target plant highlighted in green (e.g. `6.2 – 6.8 for Heirloom Tomatoes`).
   - An indicator needle drops with a gentle bounce onto the toxic level vs the fixed level.
3. **USDA Hardiness Zone Map**:
   - Clean vector map of the United States.
   - Selected cold-hardiness zones light up sequentially.
   - Text overlay shows: `Zone 5b – 8a: Cut back by Oct 15`.
4. **Before vs After / Myth vs Truth Split Screen**:
   - Vertical dividing line (`4px` Pollen Yellow) drops in center.
   - Left side (The Myth / Chemical Fix): desaturated, tinted coral, showing burnt leaves or high cost (`$45/bag`).
   - Right side (The Lazy Organic Truth): vibrant natural color, showing thriving roots and low cost (`$0 Household Fix`).

## 6. Sound & Voice Palette

- **Voice Tone**: Warm, authoritative, unhurried, reassuring American narrator (female or male "Grandma/Grandpa educator" persona).
- **Speech Rate**: **130 – 145 WPM** (TTS speed factor `0.95 – 1.02`). Do NOT use fast-talking TikTok TTS (1.2+ WPM).
- **Music Grid**:
  - Gentle acoustic folk or organic ambient pulse: acoustic guitar fingerpicking, light upright bass, soft marimba, subtle brush snare.
  - Tempo: 100–115 BPM relaxed groove (not frenetic EDM or aggressive brass).
- **Foley & Ambient Layers**:
  - Organic garden sounds: soil turning with a hand trowel, watering can drizzle, gentle wind chimes, honeybee buzz, dry leaves crunching.
  - UI Audio: Gentle wooden click for chunk slams, soft tactile page rustle for university bulletins, smooth mechanical tick for rolling counters.

---

## 7. Compliance & Safety Filters (Non-Negotiable)

- **Strict YMYL & Safety Rules**:
  - **Zero Pet Poison Risks**: Always verify against ASPCA Animal Poison Control before recommending any companion plant or spray (e.g. garlic/onion around dogs, lilies around cats).
  - **No Dangerous Chemical Hacks**: Absolutely NO bleach, kerosene, unbuffered vinegar on soil, or dangerous home fungicide recipes that violate EPA/Extension safety guidelines.
  - **Anti-Clickbait / Truth-in-Title**: Never claim "Never water your plants again" or "Secret government fertilizer". Frame as: *"The 10-Second Pruning Rule Backed by Cornell University"*.
