# Research Brief: The "Snake Map" Your Yard Doesn't Know It Has
Date: 2026-06-07
Video page: https://app.notion.com/p/377d73f5641981ab9656c34c6aa30a96

## Key Claims

- Snakes are attracted to yards that provide prey species, water sources, sunny basking areas, AND shelter — not by specific plant names alone — https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/reptiles-and-amphibians-in-your-backyard (NC State Extension)
- English ivy forms dense monoculture groundcover "suitable as habitat for rodents" — creates both rodent prey supply AND moisture/cover simultaneously — https://extension.umd.edu/resource/invasives-your-woodland-english-ivy (UMD Extension)
- Dense ground cover mats cover puddles and soggy soil, creating moisture traps and cool microclimates ideal for snakes during hot weather — https://extension.umd.edu/resource/english-ivy (UMD Extension)
- Snakes are attracted to moist, cool areas during hot weather, including watered lawns and shaded areas under decks — https://extension.oregonstate.edu/news/welcome-snakes-theyre-natural-pest-control-oregon-gardens (OSU Extension)
- Voles are prime food source for snakes (rubber boas, gopher snakes follow them into tunnels) — reducing rodent populations is key to reducing snake sightings — https://extension.umn.edu/planting-and-growing-guides/vole-damage (UMN Extension)
- Invasive grasses (dense planting) reduce environmental heterogeneity and alter snake movement patterns — https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2023.03.06.531246.full.pdf (preprint 2023 — use with hedge)

## Supporting Stats (Repellent Compounds)

- USDA Wildlife Services: Only 2 EPA-registered products for snake control (naphthalene/sulfur — Dr. T's Snake-A-Way); cinnamon oil, clove oil, and eugenol ARE effective but require direct contact — https://www.aphis.usda.gov/sites/default/files/tn_snakerepellents.pdf (USDA APHIS Tech Note 2018)
- Eugenol: moderately active as snake repellent in USDA-reviewed compound testing — same USDA APHIS source
- Citral (active compound in lemongrass) and geraniol are known to deter rodents and some insects — indirect snake deterrence via reducing prey population — [NEED DATA: peer-reviewed citation for citral's rodent-deterrent effect, ~35% foraging reduction claim]
- Alpha-terthienyl in marigold roots repels soil nematodes and reduces prey insect populations at root zone — [NEED DATA: direct peer-reviewed citation]

## YMYL Flags (cần hedge)

- Any claim about snake species identification near homes → "contact your state wildlife agency or local cooperative extension"
- Pet/child safety around plants → "refer to ASPCA Animal Poison Control for plant toxicity: aspca.org/pet-care/animal-poison-control"
- Direct snake handling or removal → YMYL — hedge: "for venomous species, always contact a professional wildlife removal service"

## Gaps (không tìm được nguồn)

- [NEED DATA] Citral from lemongrass reducing mouse foraging by ~35% — no Tier 1/2 source found, only commercial/blog claims
