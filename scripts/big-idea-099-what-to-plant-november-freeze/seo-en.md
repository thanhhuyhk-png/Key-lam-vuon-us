## YouTube SEO Description (EN)

What to plant in November depends on one number: the lowest low in your forecast. Five vegetables (kale, kohlrabi, turnips, cabbage, and spinach) sit in the hardy or moderate-freeze group on the extension tables we read. Cabbage and spinach are the weaker two, and the video says so.

You'll also see why carrots and garlic didn't make the list, how long the cold lasts matters as much as how low it gets, where November is still a planting month (mild winters) and where it's a protecting month (cold winters), and how much a row cover can add.
