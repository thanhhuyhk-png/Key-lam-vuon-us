## Research Notes (Bước 1 — web research thật, sẽ đào sâu chính thức ở [1.5])

- Eggshell/roses extension research: hiệu quả thật bị giới hạn khi rải bề mặt (phân huỷ 1-3 năm, canxi khó hấp thụ trừ đất chua) — [Ask Extension](https://ask2.extension.org/kb/faq.php?id=857460), [British Journal of Environmental Studies — eggshell CaCO3 study on Rosa](https://al-kindipublisher.com/index.php/bjes/article/view/7068)
- Coffee grounds: 1-2% nitơ, pH gần trung tính sau pha (không làm chua đất như đồn), tối đa 20% thể tích compost — [WSU Extension FS207E](https://pubs.extension.wsu.edu/product/using-coffee-grounds-in-gardens-and-landscapes-home-garden-series/), [OSU Extension](https://extension.oregonstate.edu/news/coffee-grounds-boost-soil-health-help-control-slugs)
- Cinnamon kháng nấm: ĐH Florida — giảm ~68% damping-off khi dùng đúng cách; không phải hormone kích rễ — [gardeningknowhow tổng hợp nghiên cứu](https://www.gardeningknowhow.com/garden-how-to/info/using-cinnamon-on-plants.htm)
- Root-bound/circling roots: "shave before you plant", bỏ tới 25% rễ không hại cây — [UF/IFAS Extension](https://blogs.ifas.ufl.edu/orangeco/2018/04/26/remove-circling-roots-when-planting), [Colorado State University Extension — Selecting and Planting Roses](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/selecting-and-planting-roses/)
