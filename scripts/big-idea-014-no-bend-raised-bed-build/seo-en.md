## Title
Build a No-Bend Raised Bed for Under $50

## Description
A waist-high raised bed at the garden center can cost two or three hundred dollars. But you can build one yourself for around fifty — in a single afternoon, with no power tools — so you can garden standing up and finally stop kneeling.

Using build specs from university extension services, this step-by-step shows you how: the right height and width to save your back, the false-bottom trick that slashes your soil cost, and how to fill it the old-fashioned hugelkultur way with free yard waste. We also cover which wood is actually safe for food beds, and exactly what to plant in the finished bed.

🌱 Building yours from pallets or old fence boards? Tell us in the comments.

⚠️ This video is general how-to information. There's lifting involved — if you have a back or joint condition, get help with the heavy parts or check with your doctor first.
