**The 9 Safe Plants:** Spider Plant, Cast Iron Plant, Parlor Palm, Boston Fern, Areca Palm, Peperomia, Christmas Cactus, African Violet, Prayer Plant (Calathea) — every one confirmed non-toxic by the ASPCA and its board-certified veterinary toxicologists.

**The Plant to Avoid:** Sago Palm causes the most serious risk — up to 50% of pets that eat it don't survive, and it can cause liver failure in just days. Also skip pothos, peace lily, snake plant, aloe, philodendron, and ZZ plant if you have curious pets.

**The 30-Second Trick:** Open your phone, type the plant's name + "ASPCA," and their toxic-and-non-toxic list pops right up. No guesswork needed.

**Important:** Non-toxic doesn't mean your pet should eat it. Any plant can upset a pet's stomach if they chew heavily. Keep champion chewers in sight, set plants where they look best but out of reach if needed, and call your vet or the ASPCA Animal Poison Control line if your pet swallows a big helping of anything.
