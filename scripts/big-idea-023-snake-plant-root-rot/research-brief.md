## Cau hoi 1: Co che khoa hoc gay root rot o snake plant — loai nam/vi khuan nao, tai sao dat ung nuoc tao dieu kien phat trien?

**Findings:**

- **Bon nhom tac nhan chinh** gay root rot trong cay canh va nha kinh: *Pythium* spp., *Phytophthora* spp., *Rhizoctonia solani*, va *Fusarium* spp. Ghi chu: Pythium va Phytophthora la oomycetes (water molds), khong phai nam that su, nhung thuong duoc gom chung vao nhom "root rot fungi" trong tai lieu thuc te. (Wisconsin Horticulture Extension, 2024) — https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/root-rots-houseplants/

- **Co che dat ung nuoc:** Khi dat bi ngap ung, nong do oxy trong dat giam manh, tao dieu kien ky khi (anaerobic). Cac tac nhan nhu Pythium va Phytophthora "tan dung re bi ton thuong do thieu oxy de xam nhiem." Cay rot bi nhiem benh khi re da suy yeu vi dat bao hoa nuoc. (Clemson Extension HGIC, 2024) — https://hgic.clemson.edu/hot-topic/drying-up-root-and-crown-rot-pathogens/

- **Zoospore mechanism (Pythium):** Pythium san sinh zoospores — bao tu co the boi loi trong nuoc tu do. Khi dat bao hoa nuoc (soil moisture >= 70% of available water capacity), cac bao tu nay boi duoc den re cay va xam nhiem. Khong co nuoc tu do, chung khong the di chuyen va lay benh. (UC IPM — Floriculture & Ornamental Nurseries, 2023) — https://ipm.ucanr.edu/agriculture/floriculture-and-ornamental-nurseries/pythium-root-rot/

- **Pythium dac biet nguy hiem khi dat ung lau:** "Pythium root rot can develop at any time during the growing season if the soil remains saturated for prolonged periods." Dat thoat nuoc kem, tuoi qua nhieu, la nguyen nhan pho bien. (NC State Extension, 2024) — https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/pythium-root-rot-in-turf

- **Dieu kien uu tien cua tung tac nhan** (trich Cornell Greenhouse Horticulture):
  - *Pythium*: dat lanh, am, thoat nuoc kem.
  - *Phytophthora*: dat am, thoat nuoc kem, trong cay qua sau.
  - *Rhizoctonia*: do am TRUNG BINH (khong can dat qua uot) + nhiet do am.
  - *Thielaviopsis*: pH cao, thoat nuoc kem.
  (Cornell Greenhouse Horticulture, nam khong ro — trang factsheet) — https://greenhouse.cornell.edu/pests-diseases/disease-factsheets/root-rot-diseases/

- **Southern blight (Sclerotium rolfsii):** Day la benh nam dac trung cua snake plant (Sansevieria) ben canh root rot thong thuong — gay thoi than/re o vi tri sat dat, dat am va nong la dieu kien thuan loi. (UC IPM, 2023) — https://ipm.ucanr.edu/agriculture/floriculture-and-ornamental-nurseries/southern-blight/

- **[NEED DATA: Khong tim duoc nghien cuu chuyen biet xac dinh tac nhan gay benh tren Sansevieria/snake plant o cap do .edu/.gov.** Cac nguon dang cap (.edu) neu cac tac nhan tren cho cay canh noi chung; khong co bai bao .edu/.gov isolate cu the tren Sansevieria trifasciata.]

## Cau hoi 2: Xac minh 5 sai lam cu the

### (a) Chau qua lon giu am lau — "chi nen len chau lon hon 1-2 inch"

**Findings:**

- Colorado State University Extension (PlantTalk) khuyen cao chinh xac: "choose a pot that is only one to two inches larger in diameter than the current one." (CSU PlantTalk Extension, 2024) — https://planttalk.colostate.edu/topics/houseplants/1316-houseplants-repotting/

- Ly do: Cay nho voi he re han che khong hap thu het nuoc trong the tich dat lon, dat du o trang thai am uot keo dai — day la "one of the most reliable paths to root rot in container gardens." (deepgreenpermaculture.com — nguon Tier 3, dung lam bac ly giai, co ket hop voi xac nhan tu CSU)

- **[NEED DATA: Khong tim duoc nghien cuu .edu/.gov giai thich ro co che "oversize pot → moisture retention → root rot" dac trung cho snake plant. Quy tac 1-2 inch duoc xac nhan tu CSU Extension cho cay canh noi chung, nhung khong co so lieu do luong cu the (vi du: % do am tang them bao nhieu khi chau lon hon 4 inch so voi 2 inch).]**

### (b) Nuoc dong vao tam la (rosette) gay thoi ngon/goc

**Findings:**

- Khong tim duoc nghien cuu khoa hoc chuyen biet (.edu/.gov) ve co che nuoc dong vao tam la snake plant gay thoi ngon. Day la claim thuong xuat hien trong tai lieu lam vuon pho thong nhung chua co peer-reviewed source cho Sansevieria cu the.

- **[NEED DATA: Claim "nuoc dong vao tam la gay thoi" pho bien tren gardening sites nhung thieu xac nhan tu nguon Tier 1-2. Cach tiep can an toan cho script: dien dat la "dieu kien am uot tai tam la tao moi truong thuan loi cho nam" thay vi tuyen bo co che sinh hoc chinh xac.]**

### (c) Hon hop dat ly tuong cho snake plant

**Findings:**

- Penn State Extension khuyen nghiem dung "a cactus potting mix or one with perlite" cho snake plant. (Penn State Extension, 2024) — https://extension.psu.edu/snake-plant-a-forgiving-low-maintenance-houseplant

- Ty le pho bien duoc nhieu nguon uy tin hon de cap: 60-70% cactus/succulent mix + 20-30% perlite/pumice, hoac 2 phan cactus mix : 1 phan perlite. Nguon gardening chuyentrang (getbusygardening.com) neu ty le nay; tuy nhien chua tim duoc ty le cu the tu nguon .edu/.gov.

- **[NEED DATA: Ty le pha tron chinh xac (vi du 2:1 hay 60-70% vs 30-40%) chua co xac nhan tu nguon Tier 1-2. Script chi nen noi "well-draining mix, cactus/succulent mix co them perlite" thay vi neu ty le cu the.]**

### (d) Anh sang yeu lam cham bay hoi nuoc trong dat, tang rui ro tuoi qua nhieu

**Findings:**

- Xac nhan gian tiep: "Environmental factors such as high humidity, low light, and cool temperatures can contribute to overwatering by reducing the plant's water uptake and evaporation rates." Va: "bright light dries soil quickly than shady spots, while plants in low light stay damp for longer." (Nguon: carpathen.com trich tu horticultural advice — Tier 3, can bac nhan)

- University of Maryland Extension xac nhan: "Excess water reduces oxygen in the soil, which damages fine roots." Trong do nhan thay moi quan he nguoc: anh sang tot → thoat hoi nuoc nhanh → dat kho nhanh hon. (UMD Extension, 2024) — https://extension.umd.edu/resource/overwatered-indoor-plants

- **[NEED DATA: Co so khoa hoc giai thich "low light → giam transpiration → dat giu am lau hon" la dieu hien nhien trong sinh ly thuc vat, nhung khong tim duoc nghien cuu .edu/.gov noi cu the ve snake plant va moi quan he nay. Script co the trinh bay nhu thong le thuong thuc, nhung tranh qua trinh bay nhu mot fact co so so lieu.]**

### (e) Tan suat tuoi nuoc dung

**Findings:**

- Penn State Extension: tuoi khi dat kho — co the bo qua 1 thang khong tuoi ma khong gay hai. "Overwatering is the primary threat." (Penn State Extension, 2024) — https://extension.psu.edu/snake-plant-a-forgiving-low-maintenance-houseplant

- Kha nang dung so lieu: nhieu nguon (Tier 3) neu 2-6 tuan/lan tuy nhiet do, do am, mua, kich thuoc chau. Mua dong co the giam con 1 lan/thang. Day la range thuc te nhung den tu nguon gardening thuong mai.

- **[NEED DATA: Khong tim duoc nghien cuu .edu/.gov neu cu the tan suat tuoi cho Sansevieria (vi du X lan/tuan o nhiet do Y do F). Script nen dung "check khi 2 inch dat tren mat da kho han" thay vi neu so tuan cu the.]**

## Cau hoi 3: Quy trinh cuu cay bi thoi re (rescue protocol)

**Findings:**

- **Nguon uy tin nhat tim duoc (NYBG Mertz Library — Tier 2/3):** Quy trinh day du gom:
  1. Kiem tra muc do thiet hai: neu phan than tren mat dat (crown) da mem/co mui hoi → nen bo cay.
  2. Lay cay ra khoi chau, xuc dat nhe bang nuoc am de thay ro re.
  3. Dung dao da khu trung, cat bo tat ca re den/nhun; chi giu phan re khoe.
  4. Trong lai trong chau da ve sinh, vua du chua he re, dat moi thoat nuoc nhanh (succulent mix).
  (NYBG Mertz Library Reference, 2024) — https://libanswers.nybg.org/faq/396986

- **Wisconsin Horticulture Extension** (Tier 1) co quan diem don gian hon: phuong an hieu qua nhat thuong la "vut cay di"; neu giu thi "giam do am dat" — khong de cap den buoc cat re cu the. Khong khuyen dung thuoc nam hoa hoc cho cay trong nha vi it san pham phu hop va dat. (Wisc. Horticulture Extension, 2024) — https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/root-rots-houseplants/

- **Ask Extension (Tier 1 — NIFA network):** Loi khuyen tap trung vao: bo la hu, giu dat "evenly moist (not too dry or too wet)", dung chau co lo thoat nuoc, KHONG dat vat lieu (da, manh vo) duoi day chau vi thuc ra can tro thoat nuoc. (Ask Extension / NIFA, 2024) — https://ask.extension.org/kb/faq.php?id=780324

- **Cac buoc co the thieu trong script:**
  - Nen de he re da cat bo "air dry" 30-60 phut truoc khi trong lai (de vet cat dong lai — callous) — nguon Tier 3 nen dieu nay, phu hop voi nguyen tac sinh ly nhung chua duoc xac nhan bang .edu/.gov.
  - Khu trung cong cu bang dung dich bleach 10% hoac alcohol — xac nhan tu UMD Extension. (UMD Extension, 2024) — https://extension.umd.edu/resource/overwatered-indoor-plants
  - Bo het dat cu (co the chua nam); trong lai bang dat moi hoan toan.
  - Cho 5-7 ngay sau khi trong lai moi bat dau tuoi.

- **[NEED DATA: Khong co nghien cuu .edu/.gov xac nhan thoi gian de vet cat kho chinh xac (30 phut, 1 gio, hay 24 gio) cho Sansevieria. Nganh nguyen tac sinh ly ho tro viec de kho vet cat truoc khi trong, nhung con so cu the la chua duoc kiem chung.]**

## Cau hoi 4: Cinnamon claim — khang nam that khong? Co phai rooting hormone khong?

**Findings:**

- **Tinh khang nam co nen tang khoa hoc:** Nghien cuu peer-reviewed xac nhan:
  - Chieu xuat cinnamomum bark o nong do 5% uc che *Fusarium sporotrichioides* 70% sau 7 ngay; nong do 3% chi uc che 10.4%. (Pubmed/PMC, Antoniolli et al., 2025) — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11740748/
  - Cinnamon water filtrate o 0.5% uc che *Botrytis cinerea* (gray mold) 54.4%; o 1% uc che 81.4% sau 6 ngay. (European Journal of Plant Pathology, 2019) — https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10658-019-01882-0 [Note: khong truoc tiep fetch duoc PDF vi paywall; so lieu xac nhan tu tom tat cong khai va search result]

- **Tinh khang nam la trong phong thi nghiem (in vitro):** Cac nghien cuu tren dung chiet xuat/dung dich — KHONG phai cinnamon bot kho rai len vet cat. Hieu qua trong thuc te nha vuon co the thap hon dang ke. Script nen hedge: "cinnamon co tinh khang nam duoc chung minh trong phong thi nghiem."

- **Cinnamon KHONG phai rooting hormone — xac dinh ro rang:**
  - Cinnamon KHONG chua auxin — loai hormone kich thich ra re that su.
  - "Cinnamon has no documented hormonal influence on stem cuttings." No la chat khang khuan/khang nam, bao ve vet cat khoi nhiem trung, khong phai kich thich cay ra re.
  - Cac nghien cuu dung chiet xuat cinnamon voi bot lan Botrytis (European Journal Plant Pathology, 2019) cho thay co tac dung ky sinh tren nam, nhung khong co chuyen gia nao xac nhan co hoat tinh auxin.
  (Tong hop: PMC 2025 + EJPP 2019 + khao sat cac nguon horticultural)

- **Khac biet quan trong cho script:**
  - DUNG: "Cinnamon has antifungal properties that may help protect cut surfaces from fungal infection."
  - SAI: "Cinnamon helps roots grow faster" / "Cinnamon stimulates root development."
  - HEDGE CAN THIET: "Some gardeners use cinnamon powder on cut root ends — it has documented antifungal properties in lab studies, though it is not a rooting hormone and will not speed up root regrowth."

- **[NEED DATA: Khong tim duoc nghien cuu .edu/.gov kiem tra hieu qua cinnamon bot (dang kho, khong phai dung dich) ap truc tiep len vet cat re Sansevieria. Hieu qua trong thuc te con la cau hoi mo.]**

## Cau hoi 5: Snake plant co doc voi thu cung (cho/meo) khong?

**Findings:**

- **ASPCA xac nhan ro rang:** Snake plant (Sansevieria trifasciata) la "Toxic to Dogs, Toxic to Cats."
  - **Doc chat:** Saponins (steroidal saponins).
  - **Trieu chung:** Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea (buon non, non muoi, tieu chay).
  - **Duong khau: (888) 426-4435** (ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center).
  (ASPCA, 2024) — https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/aspca-poison-control/toxic-and-non-toxic-plants/snake-plant

- **ASPCA cung xac nhan tuong tu** voi ten goi "Mother-in-Law's Tongue": cung Saponins, cung trieu chung. (ASPCA, 2024) — https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/aspca-poison-control/toxic-and-non-toxic-plants/mother-laws-tongue

- **Muc do nghiem trong:** Saponins pha vo mang te bao ruot, gay roi loan tieu hoa. "Toxic" nhung khong o cap "potentially fatal" doi voi vat nuoi binh thuong tru khi an qua nhieu. Tuy nhien, khi cuu cay (dao chau, cat re, de re cat nam ngoai), phan re/la bi cat la dac biet de tiep can — NEU CAN CANH BAO trong script noi "keep pets away while repotting."

- [YMYL] **Neu thu cung an bat ky phan nao cua snake plant, lien he ASPCA Poison Control (888) 426-4435 hoac bac sy thu y ngay.**
