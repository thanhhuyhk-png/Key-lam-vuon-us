## ⚠️ IMPORTANT: Safety & Garden Care

**Rooting Powder:** Read the label on your product. Keep it away from children and pets, and wear gloves when handling. If the powder touches your skin or eyes, follow the first-aid directions on the label. If you have questions, contact your local extension office.

**Pets & Roses:** The ASPCA lists roses (genus *Rosa*) as non-toxic to dogs, cats, and horses. However, a plant with "rose" in its name might not be a true rose—check the Latin name on your plant tag. Rose thorns can prick. Rooting powder should be kept away from pets and children. If a pet swallows rooting powder or cuttings, contact your veterinarian.
