## PHƯƠNG ÁN A — Data / Statement (⭐ khuyến nghị)
**Type:** Data/Shock · **Lý do:** kết quả đo giao ngay (<0:15), loop về "kết quả thứ hai" còn sống tới cuối hook và sang M1.

### Spoken Hook (~105 từ ≈ 0:38 @165wpm)
> Push a rose cutting into a potato, and the roots show up overnight. That's what the videos say. One small study actually tried it. The cuttings in the potatoes grew no roots. Most of the ones in plain soil did.
>
> So should you toss the potato? Maybe. But that same study held a second result, and it's about the cutting itself. Get that part wrong, and you could wait weeks on a stick that was never going to root. Get it right, and a few cuttings, a pot, some mix and a bag is all you need.
>
> First, why the potato trick sounds so convincing.

### Spoken Hook (~86 từ ≈ 0:31)
> Have you ever pushed a rose cutting into half a potato, hoping for roots by morning? One small study tried it. Not one potato cutting grew a root. Most of the cuttings in plain soil did.

> But the potato wasn't the biggest finding. Tucked in that same paper is a second result, about the cutting itself, and it's the one the authors could back up with numbers. It decides what you cut, and what you throw away.
>
> You'll want to hear it before your next cutting.
