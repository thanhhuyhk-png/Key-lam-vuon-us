# Thumbnail Prompts — Why Afternoon Sun Kills Aloe Plants—And the Biology of Where to Move It

**Nguồn công thức:** `references/thumbnail-stats-garden-us-60k.md` (AUDIT TL;DR, 14 thumbnail teardown) — GHI ĐÈ concept generic gốc ở `outline.md` ("SCORCHED → LUSH" chữ trắng thuần) vì kênh có công thức thật: **2 tầng chữ, hook VÀNG + phụ TRẮNG**, không phải trắng thuần.

**Chế độ A/B:** kênh đang trong **Browse demotion** (channel-stats-garden-us-60k.md) → **MODE 1 — Concept Test** bắt buộc (A/B khác hẳn đòn tâm lý, KHÔNG chỉ đổi 1 biến nhỏ).

**Số trên thumb:** không dùng con số cụ thể — layout Before→After không cần số, và "3 to 5 feet" đã là hook của title/hook video, tránh lặp 3 lần (title → hook → thumb) làm mất Complementarity.

**Complementarity Test:** content words A = {WRONG, SPOT, BURNS, ALOE} — chỉ "ALOE" trùng title (chủ thể bắt buộc, cho phép). B = {SCORCHED, NOT, DISEASE} — 0 từ trùng title. ✅ PASS cả 2.

**Lever Value Equation:** Title kéo *Cảm giác chắc thắng↑ (Biology giải thích) + Kết quả↑ (Where to Move It)*. A kéo *Chắc-thắng↑ khác góc* (phán quyết rõ đúng/sai — nhìn phát biết ngay). B kéo *Curiosity thuần* (đặt câu hỏi ngược lại giả định "sun-loving = an toàn"). Cả 2 đều KHÔNG lặp lever của title → bổ trợ đúng nguyên tắc 1+1=3.

```
A photorealistic 16:9 YouTube thumbnail for a US home-gardening channel, audience 50–80. Vivid saturated colors, ultra high contrast, blurred sunny windowsill garden background. NO human face, no full person.

LAYOUT: a clean before→after split, two halves, the right half visually dominant (roughly 60% of the frame) and brighter/sharper than the left.

LEFT HALF (the wrong way, ~40% of frame, slightly darker/duller): a potted aloe vera plant sitting directly against a sun-drenched west-facing window, its leaves showing bronze, reddish-brown, dry and crispy sunburned patches. A bold red dashed circle around the worst burned patch, and a large red ❌ symbol overlaid on this half.

RIGHT HALF (the fix, ~60% of frame, bright and vivid): a healthy potted aloe vera plant with plump, glossy, deep-green leaves, positioned a few feet back from a bright window with soft, indirect light. A bright green ✅ symbol on this half.

TEXT — render EXACTLY two stacked words in very thick bold sans-serif, heavy black outline + drop shadow, centered in the negative space, NOT covering the plants: line 1 "WRONG SPOT" in bright YELLOW, line 2 "BURNS ALOE" in WHITE. The ONLY text in the entire image is exactly these two lines — spelled letter for letter, nothing else, no other letters, numbers, or words anywhere.

Negative: no face, no full person, no extra text, no gibberish letters, no watermark, no logo, no UI, no distorted shapes, no snake or rodent imagery.
```

## MASTER PROMPT B — Curiosity / Single-Hero

```
A photorealistic 16:9 YouTube thumbnail for a US home-gardening channel, audience 50–80. Vivid saturated colors, ultra high contrast, blurred sunny windowsill garden background. NO human face, no full person.

SCENE: ONE single hero object only — an extreme macro close-up of a sunburned aloe vera leaf, showing sharp contrast between healthy deep-green tissue and bronze/reddish-brown crispy dry burned patches. The leaf is offset to one side of the frame, large and in sharp focus, leaving clean negative space on the other side. A bold red question mark "?" floats near the burned patch, and a curved yellow arrow points directly at the worst-damaged area. Shallow depth of field, soft warm window light in the background.

TEXT — render EXACTLY two stacked words in very thick bold sans-serif, heavy black outline + drop shadow, placed in the negative space, NOT covering the leaf: line 1 "SCORCHED?" in bright YELLOW, line 2 "NOT DISEASE" in WHITE. The ONLY text in the entire image is exactly these two lines — spelled letter for letter, nothing else, no other letters, numbers, or words anywhere.

Negative: no face, no full person, no second object, no extra text, no gibberish letters, no watermark, no logo, no UI, no distorted shapes, no snake or rodent imagery.
```

## Ghi chú sản xuất
- Chữ baked sai dai dẳng sau 3 lần render → rút ngắn còn 1 từ/dòng (vd A: "WRONG" / "SPOT!"), giữ ảnh hoàn chỉnh từ prompt, KHÔNG chuyển sang overlay Canva.
- Nguồn số: không có con số nào trên thumb — không áp dụng zero-hallucination check cho số liệu ở bước này.
- A/B là MODE 1 (concept test) vì kênh đang Browse demotion — impression thấp nên đọc kết quả Test & Compare thận trọng, ưu tiên retention/Spine hơn optimize thumbnail lúc này (theo thumbnail-stats-garden-us-60k.md dòng 80).
