# Thumbnail Prompts — 3 Signs Your Aloe Vera Is About to Bloom (Don't Miss It)

**Mode:** MODE 1 — Concept Test (bắt buộc, kênh đang Browse demotion → impression thấp, isolation test vô nghĩa)
**Cặp archetype:** Verdict (Before→After) × Curiosity (single-hero) — khác đòn tâm lý rõ
**Title lever đã kéo:** Kết quả↑ ("3 Signs...Bloom") + Thời gian↓ ("Don't Miss It")
**Số trên thumb:** không dùng số nào — không có số nào bịa (đúng zero-hallucination)

---

## Bảng biến thể

| | Variant A — Verdict | Variant B — Curiosity |
|---|---|---|
| Concept | Before→After: pup ❌ vs cuống hoa thật ✅ | Single-hero: cận cảnh mũi giáo cuống hoa mới nhú tại tâm bụi |
| Layout WIN | ① Before→After split | ② Single hero + text negative space |
| Text | "THE SIGN" (vàng) / "NOT A PUP" (trắng) | "LOOK CLOSER" (vàng) / "AT THE CENTER" (trắng) |
| Lever Value Eq (khác title) | Cảm giác chắc thắng↑ (phân biệt được, không nhầm) | Công sức↓ (chỉ cần nhìn kỹ) |
| Cảm-xúc-đích (G4) | Verdict/certainty — "à, hiểu rồi" | Tò mò — "cái gì đang xảy ra ở đó?" |
| Markup | Khoanh đỏ nét đứt quanh pup + ❌ đỏ trái · ✅ xanh phải, bên phải to/rực hơn ~60/40 | Mũi tên cong vàng trỏ vào đầu mũi giáo, ánh sáng vàng ấm phá nền xanh |
| Complementarity | 0 từ trùng title | 0 từ trùng title |
| GURU 5G | G1✅(hook≤2 từ/dòng) G2✅(phải dominant 60/40) G3✅(chưa lộ 2 sign còn lại) G4✅ G5✅(đỏ/xanh phá nền xanh) | G1✅ G2✅(1 hero lệch, blur nền) G3✅(chưa lộ đó là gì) G4✅(vật thể lạ = cảm xúc) G5✅(ánh sáng vàng ấm phá nền xanh) |

## MASTER PROMPT A — Verdict (Before→After)

```
A photorealistic 16:9 YouTube thumbnail for a US home-gardening channel, audience 50-80. Vivid saturated colors, ultra high contrast, blurred sunny windowsill background. NO human face, no full person.

LAYOUT: a clean before-after split, two halves, RIGHT half visually dominant (about 60% of frame width), LEFT half smaller (about 40%).

LEFT HALF (the wrong guess, smaller, slightly desaturated): a small baby aloe vera pup, a tiny rosette of thick green leaves, growing low at the base of a mature aloe plant, at soil level, in a terracotta pot. A bold red dashed circle around this small pup and a large red X mark over it.

RIGHT HALF (the real sign, larger, bright and vivid): a pale green almost-white spear-shaped flower stalk rising straight up from the exact center of a large healthy aloe vera rosette, stiff and upright, morning light behind it. A bright green checkmark on this side.

TEXT — render EXACTLY two stacked words in very thick bold sans-serif, heavy black outline and drop shadow, centered in the lower third, NOT covering the plants: line 1 "THE SIGN" in bright YELLOW, line 2 "NOT A PUP" in WHITE.
Do NOT add any other text, words, letters, numbers, captions, labels, or watermark.

Negative: no face, no full person, no extra text, no gibberish letters, no watermark, no logo, no UI, no distorted shapes, no threatening animals.
```

## MASTER PROMPT B — Curiosity (single-hero)

```
A photorealistic 16:9 YouTube thumbnail for a US home-gardening channel, audience 50-80. Vivid saturated colors, ultra high contrast, warm golden-hour light, blurred sunny windowsill background. NO human face, no full person.

SCENE: ONE single hero object only — an extreme macro close-up of the very center of a large healthy aloe vera rosette, where a pale green, almost-white, spear-shaped bud is just emerging straight up from the exact middle of the thick green leaves. Shallow depth of field, the bud in sharp focus, leaves softly blurred around it. The hero is offset to one side of the frame, leaving clean negative space on the other side for text. A bold curved yellow arrow points directly at the emerging bud.

TEXT — render EXACTLY two stacked words in very thick bold sans-serif, heavy black outline and drop shadow, placed in the negative space, NOT covering the plant: line 1 "LOOK CLOSER" in bright YELLOW, line 2 "AT THE CENTER" in WHITE.
Do NOT add any other text, words, letters, numbers, captions, labels, or watermark.

Negative: no face, no full person, no second object, no extra text, no gibberish letters, no watermark, no logo, no UI, no distorted shapes.
```
