## Cau hoi 1: Trong 7 cay, cay nao song LAU DAI (months/years) chi trong nuoc, cay nao chi propagate tam roi can chuyen sang dat?

**Findings:**

**LONG-TERM WATER GROWERS (months to years — documented):**

- **Pothos (Epipremnum aureum):** Co the song vinh vien trong nuoc neu duoc bo sung dinh duong. Gardening Know How xac nhan day la "hydroculture" that su — khong phai propagation tam thoi — mien la nhan liquid fertilizer moi 4-6 tuan va thay nuoc hoan toan moi 2-3 tuan. (Gardening Know How, n.d.) — https://www.gardeningknowhow.com/houseplants/pothos/pothos-in-water.htm
- **Pothos documented case:** Mot tac gia ghi nhan trong pothos trong nuoc lien tuc TREN 3 NAM, cay dai hon 1.5m, ra la moi lien tuc, dung nuoc pha vai giot phan bon long moi thang trong lo thuy tinh. (Plant House and Garden, n.d.) — https://planthouseandgarden.com/growing-golden-pothos-in-water-for-3-years/
- **Lucky Bamboo (Dracaena sanderiana):** Arkansas Cooperative Extension xac nhan "de giu trong nuoc 1 nam tro len" nhung cuoi cung can bo sung dinh duong (phan long pha loang moi vai thang). Clemson Extension khuyen thay nuoc hang tuan, bon phan moi 2 thang voi 1/4 lieu khuyen nghi. (Arkansas Cooperative Extension, n.d.) — https://www.uaex.uada.edu/yard-garden/resource-library/plant-week/lucky-bamboo.aspx | (Clemson Extension, n.d.) — https://hgic.clemson.edu/how-to-grow-and-care-for-lucky-bamboo-dracaena-sanderiana/

**PROPAGATION-ONLY / TIEN TRIEN HAN CHE (khong phai long-term viable trong nuoc thua thuong):**

- **Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum):** Gardening Know How xac nhan ro rang: "Plants need certain nutrients to grow and thrive, and can't be sustained in water long term unless you are using a hydroponic solution. Growing a spider plant in water is a first step to starting a new plant but not a sustainable system." Re trong nuoc chi tot de propagation (7-10 ngay bat dau ra re, 1 thang re dai 5-7 cm), sau do nen chuyen sang dat. (Gardening Know How, n.d.) — https://www.gardeningknowhow.com/houseplants/spider-plant/spider-plants-in-water.htm
- **Heartleaf Philodendron (Philodendron hederaceum):** Nguon khong co du lieu tu extension/.edu xac nhan long-term water culture. Cach pho bien la propagation trung gian, sau do chuyen dat. Nhieu nguon non-authoritative cho rang philodendron co the song trong nuoc lau — nhung khong co extension publication xac nhan. [NEED DATA: Peer-reviewed or extension confirmation of heartleaf philodendron long-term water culture viability]
- **Coleus (Plectranthus scutellarioides):** Gardening blog va forum cho rang coleus song duoc "vai thang" trong nuoc va co the overwinter hydroponically, nhung "kho giu mai mai" va "cuoi cung nen cho vao dat de song lau dai." Mau sac la co the nhat. Khong co extension source xac nhan long-term permanence. [NEED DATA: Extension/university confirmation of coleus permanent water culture viability]
- **Tradescantia / Wandering Jew (Tradescantia zebrina):** UF/IFAS ghi nhan propagation de dang bang stem cuttings trong nuoc nhung khong xac nhan water culture permanence. OSU Extension khong accessible. Con vat lieu chi chi la propagation method. [NEED DATA: Extension confirmation of Tradescantia long-term water culture]
- **Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum spp.):** Bright Lane Gardens xac nhan chi co the song lau dai trong nuoc neu co HE THONG HYDROPONIC THAT SU (dung dich dinh duong EC 0.8-1.2 mS/cm, anh sang 12-16 gio/ngay, kiem soat pH 6.0-6.5). Mot lo nuoc thua trong phong khach khong du — se thieu dinh duong va co the bi rot. SDSU Extension khong de cap den water growing. (Bright Lane Gardens, 2023) — https://brightlanegardens.com/indoor-garden/hydroponics/grow-a-peace-lily-hydroponic/

## Cau hoi 2: Coleus co giu duoc lau dai trong nuoc khong, va can bo sung dinh duong gi (compost tea / phan pha loang) — ti le & tan suat cu the?

**Findings:**

- Coleus co the song trong nuoc nhieu thang khi cat canh, dac biet de overwinter, nhung "kho giu mai mai" va "cuoi cung nen cho vao dat." (Gardens Whisper, n.d.) — https://gardenswhisper.com/can-coleus-grow-in-water/
- Tan suat thay nuoc: Moi 4-6 ngay, hoac som hon neu nuoc duc. Nguon gardening blog khuyen ca 4-6 ngay / khi duc. (Gardens Whisper, n.d.) — https://gardenswhisper.com/can-coleus-grow-in-water/
- Dinh duong: Vi coleus trong nuoc khong co dinh duong dat, can bo sung phan long khi thay nuoc. Khuyen nghi: balanced water-soluble fertilizer (NPK 10-10-10 hoac 20-20-20) PHA LOANG BANG MOT NUA LIEU (half-strength) moi 2-4 tuan trong mua tang truong. (Gardens Whisper, n.d.) — https://gardenswhisper.com/can-coleus-grow-in-water/
- Khong co extension/.edu publication nao xac nhan ti le phan bon cu the cho coleus trong nuoc hoac xac nhan viability lau dai. [NEED DATA: Extension/university coleus water culture fertilizer ratio confirmation]
- Luu y: Khi trong nuoc, la coleus co the mat mau (mat sac sang). Can anh sang day du (cua so huong dong/tay) de giu mau la.

**Ket luan cho script:** Coleus trong nuoc duoc nhieu nguon xac nhan la TEMPORARY/SEASONAL (vai thang den 1 nam), khong phai permanent long-term plant. An toan khi say: "Coleus thrives in water for several months — perfect for overwintering — but eventually does best when moved to a pot."

## Cau hoi 3: Peace Lily co THAT SU ra hoa khi trong chi trong nuoc khong, va can dieu kien gi?

**Findings:**

- **UF/IFAS (peer-reviewed extension publication, 2003):** Ra hoa o Spathiphyllum duoc kich hoat boi: (a) Nhiet do: Optimal 22°C (72°F) cho loai seasonal; chill xuong 12°C (54°F) co the cho ra hoa som 3-4 tuan. (b) Gibberellic acid (GA3): Mot lan phun la co the cho ra hoa sau 70-110 ngay. (c) Quang chu ky: KHONG CO loai nao duoc xac nhan la photoperiod-responsive trong nghien cuu nay. (UF/IFAS Extension — Spathiphyllum Flowering, 2003) — https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/EP320
- **Clemson Extension:** "Thieu anh sang la nguyen nhan chinh khien cay khong ra hoa." Can anh sang gian tiep sang de khich hoa. (Clemson Extension, n.d.) — https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/peace-lily/
- **Arkansas Cooperative Extension:** Peace lily trong dieu kien thong thuong "chi ra it hoa vao thoi diem khong du doan truoc." Can du sang de ra hoa (it nhat du de doc bao). (Arkansas Cooperative Extension, n.d.) — https://www.uaex.uada.edu/yard-garden/resource-library/plant-week/peace-lily.aspx
- **Hydroponic blooming:** Bright Lane Gardens (horticulture resource, 2023) ghi nhan peace lily CO THE ra hoa khi trong nuoc NHUNG chi voi he thong hydroponic that su: anh sang 12-16 gio/ngay (blue + red spectrum), dung dich dinh duong EC 0.8-1.2, pH 6.0-6.5. Chi can nuoc trong lo thua khong dam bao ra hoa. (Bright Lane Gardens, 2023) — https://brightlanegardens.com/indoor-garden/hydroponics/grow-a-peace-lily-hydroponic/
- **SDSU Extension:** Xac nhan peace lily "ra hoa trang, lau, dac sac va co mui huong nhe" nhung chi mo ta trong moi truong dat; de nghi nuoc khong co clo (de qua dem truoc khi tuoi). (SDSU Extension, n.d.) — https://extension.sdstate.edu/peace-lily-houseplant-how

**Ket luan cho script:** Peace lily CAN ra hoa trong nuoc nhung KHO hon trong dat va can dieu kien khac khat (anh sang du, dinh duong, pH). Voi lo nuoc gian di trong phong, nguoi xem CO THE thay la xanh dep nhung HOA la thuong hiem hon. An toan khi say: "Peace lily can surprise you with a bloom — but it needs bright indirect light and monthly plant food. Without that, you'll still get gorgeous dark green leaves."

## Cau hoi 5: Tan suat doi nuoc cho cay thuy sinh trong nha, va co can khu clo / dung nuoc loc thay nuoc may khong?

**Findings:**

**Tan suat doi nuoc:**
- Pothos trong nuoc: Thay nuoc hoan toan (khong chi thet them) moi 2-3 tuan. "Don't just top off — dump it out and replace it completely" vi oxygen trong nuoc can kiet theo thoi gian. (Gardening Know How, n.d.) — https://www.gardeningknowhow.com/houseplants/pothos/pothos-in-water.htm
- Illinois Extension (vase flowers general): Thay nuoc moi 2-3 ngay hoac khi nuoc duc. (Illinois Extension, 2023) — https://extension.illinois.edu/blogs/flowers-fruits-and-frass/2023-02-10-extend-vase-life-your-fresh-flowers-little-care
- Lucky Bamboo: Clemson Extension khuyen thay nuoc hang tuan. (Clemson Extension, n.d.) — https://hgic.clemson.edu/how-to-grow-and-care-for-lucky-bamboo-dracaena-sanderiana/
- Coleus: Thay nuoc moi 4-6 ngay hoac khi nuoc duc. (Gardens Whisper, n.d.) — https://gardenswhisper.com/can-coleus-grow-in-water/
- Peace lily hydroponic: Thay nuoc moi 1-2 tuan hoac khi duc. (Bright Lane Gardens, 2023) — https://brightlanegardens.com/indoor-garden/hydroponics/grow-a-peace-lily-hydroponic/

**Clo va Fluor trong nuoc may:**
- **MSU Extension** xac nhan: Fluor trong nuoc may gay "necrotic regions, especially at the tips and along margins of leaves" tren cac loai nhay cam, dac biet MOT LA CAY (monocots) bao gom spider plant, lilies, va dracaena. Bien phap: dung nuoc gieng, nuoc mua, hoac loc RO. "Once damage appears, the damage is irreversible." (MSU Extension, n.d.) — https://www.canr.msu.edu/news/fluoride_toxicity_in_plants_irrigated_with_city_water
- **Iowa State Extension** xac nhan: "Too much chlorine and other chemicals in water, such as fluoride, can cause brown leaf tips" tren spider plant, dracaena, prayer plant va calathea. Khuyen nghi: "Use a different water source if this issue is suspected." (Iowa State Extension, n.d.) — https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/faq/why-does-my-houseplant-have-brown-leaf-tips-and-edges
- **UMD Extension** khuyen: De nuoc may qua dem (24 gio trong lo mo) truoc khi dung cho cay, de clo bay hoi. Luu y: chloramine (chat khu trung hien dai hon) KHONG bay hoi nhu clo — can loc RO de loai bo hoan toan. (UMD Extension, n.d.) — https://extension.umd.edu/resource/watering-indoor-plants
- **Arkansas Cooperative Extension** (lucky bamboo): Gay "leaf tip burn" do muoi hoac fluor cao trong nuoc. Khuyen dung nuoc mua de tranh van de nay. (Arkansas Cooperative Extension, n.d.) — https://www.uaex.uada.edu/yard-garden/resource-library/plant-week/lucky-bamboo.aspx

**Tom tat hanh dong thuc te cho nguoi dung:**
1. Thay nuoc hoan toan (khong thet them) moi 1-2 tuan doi voi chau nuoc trong phong.
2. Neu co dau hieu dau la (brown tips) o spider plant, dracaena/lucky bamboo, hoan phan tiep hoac chuyen sang nuoc loc / nuoc mua.
3. De nuoc may qua dem 24h truoc khi dung se loai mot phan clo; nhung khong loai duoc chloramine hoac fluor — nuoc loc la tot hon.
