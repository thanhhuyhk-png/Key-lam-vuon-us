### PARADIGM SHIFT (1–2 phút)
- Snakes cần 3 thứ: cover từ predators, moisture regulation, prey supply.
- Bất kỳ cây nào tạo ra 3 thứ đó đều là snake habitat — bất kể tên gì.
- Visual on-screen: diagram "3 snake needs → microclimate map"

### ZONE 1 — Foundation Perimeter (2–4 phút)
**Vấn đề:** Ground cover dày sát nhà
- English ivy: cool, damp mat → snake bunker
- Dense liriope border: snake highway dọc móng nhà
- Thick mulch >3 inches: insulation cho rắn qua đông

**Fix:**
- Remove/thin ground cover within 12–18 inches of foundation
- Replace with open gravel border
- Trim shrubs so first branch ≥6 inches above ground (daylight test)
- Rosemary/lemongrass clumps AT corners — not massed

**Data:** Snakes prefer cover keeping them within 2–3°F of optimal body temp (78–80°F for most US species).

### ZONE 2 — Mid-Garden (4–6 phút)
**Vấn đề:** Shrubs + ornamental grasses = shelter + prey magnet
- Pampas grass, maiden grass: dense base = nesting site
- Thick juniper with low branches = snake hotel
- Star jasmine: attracts rodents → attracts snakes
- Wood/leaf piles: hibernation site

**Fix:**
- "Lift the skirt": prune bottom 6–8 inches of all shrubs
- Replace mass ornamental grass with separated clumps + airy flowers between
- Remove debris piles within 20 feet of house
- Mow short any grass near garden edges

### ZONE 3 — Border & Fence Line (6–8 phút)
**Vấn đề:** Fence line = migration corridor
**Cơ hội:** Tạo active deterrent — "Living Fence"

- **Rosemary hedge:** camphor + 1,8-cineole → volatile aversive compounds (same as commercial repellents). Plant 2–3 feet spacing; prune to keep dense above, open at base.
- **Lemongrass wall:** citral → olfactory barrier; 4–6 feet tall, clump form. Zone 9b–11 perennial; Zone 8 and below: annual or container.
- **Marigold border:** alpha-terthienyl → repels insects + rodents (removes prey). Plant 6 inches apart along fence edge.

**Key rule:** Living fence works by SCENT RELEASE, not mass coverage. Keep base OPEN.
