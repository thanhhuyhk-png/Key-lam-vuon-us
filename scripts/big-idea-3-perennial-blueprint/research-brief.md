### Câu 2: Catmint Rebloom Sau Khi Cắt

**Trả lời:** CONFIRMED — cắt back 1/3 đến 1/2 sau đợt bloom đầu → reblooms. Nhưng "6 weeks" là không có nguồn xác nhận cụ thể.

Nguyên văn NC State: *"The flowers are sterile with no seed production and will rebloom if cut back mid-summer."*
Nguyên văn Wisconsin Extension: *"Cutting back plants by half to a third after the initial flush of flowers fade will promote reblooming and a more attractive appearance later in the season."*

**Kết luận cho script:** Nói "cut back by 1/3 in mid-summer after first bloom → reblooms within weeks." KHÔNG hardcode "6 weeks" vì chưa có nguồn xác nhận số này.

**Nguồn:**
- [NC State Extension — Walker's Low](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/nepeta-x-faassenii-walkers-low/)
- [Wisconsin Horticulture — Nepeta x faassenii](https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/nepeta-x-faassenii-walkers-low/)

### Câu 4: Spacing + Canopy Closure — Weed Suppression

**Trả lời:** Mechanism CONFIRMED — tighter planting suppresses weeds by blocking light to soil. 

Key data:
- "A slightly tighter planting fills in faster and suppresses weeds during the **first two growing seasons**" (Nature Hills)
- Narrow row/plant spacing → faster canopy closure → less light → fewer weed seedlings (NCBI research)
- 28 plants ở spacing 18-30 inches trong 100 sq ft: phù hợp với design principles. Số học: 100 sq ft ÷ (18in × 18in per plant) ≈ 44 slots (max). 28 plants = moderate density với mulch in gaps.

**Correction cho script:** Outline nói "Year 3: canopy closes." Nguồn nói "first two growing seasons." Nên điều chỉnh thành **"Year 2-3 canopy closes"** thay vì chỉ Year 3.

**Nguồn:**
- [Live to Plant — Spacing to Minimize Weeds](https://livetoplant.com/how-to-space-and-place-plants-to-minimize-weed-growth/)
- [Nature Hills — Plant Spacing Guide](https://naturehills.com/blogs/garden-blog/how-to-space-plants-like-a-pro-smart-plant-placement-tips)
