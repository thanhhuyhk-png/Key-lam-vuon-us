## DESCRIPTION

Grow vegetables indoors this winter: which of seven can try a bright window, and which two need a lamp. This video lines up what university extension offices say about winter light, seven vegetables, lamps, and common problems, and it's upfront where the sources go quiet.

**In this video:**
- What a winter window gives you, and the quiet sign your plants send when it isn't enough
- Five to try on a bright window: microgreens, baby greens, leaf lettuce, chives and parsley, radishes
- Two that need extra light in winter: tomatoes and peppers, and why a tomato's lamp needs a dark break
- A table of the extension numbers, plus three places the universities disagree
- Lamp distance, hours, a timer, and a 4-week plan

Tomorrow's one-day test: tape a sheet of paper by your window and write "sun" or "no sun" each hour. Compare it to the six to eight hours extension guides give for lettuce and herbs.
