## MASTER PROMPT A (Before→After — Verdict)

```
A photorealistic 16:9 YouTube thumbnail for a US home-gardening channel, audience 50–80. Vivid saturated colors, ultra high contrast, blurred garden background. NO human face, no full person (a single hand as prop is allowed only if described).

LAYOUT: a clean before→after split, two halves, the right half slightly more dominant and brighter than the left.
LEFT HALF (the wrong way): a garden hose pouring water directly onto the base of a climbing clematis vine, the soil right at the stem dark, muddy, and waterlogged. A bold red dashed circle around the soggy soil at the stem, and a large red ❌ over it.
RIGHT HALF (the fix): the same clematis vine, healthy and vibrant with lush green leaves, a thin soaker hose looped in a ring several inches out from the stem, the soil at the stem itself dry and lightly mulched, the outer ring visibly moist. A bright green ✅ on this side.

TEXT — render EXACTLY two stacked words in very thick bold sans-serif, heavy black outline and drop shadow, centered in the negative space, NOT covering the main objects: line 1 "DEAD ZONE" in bright YELLOW, line 2 "AT THE STEM" in WHITE. The ONLY text in the entire image is exactly these two lines — spelled letter for letter, nothing else, no other letters, numbers, or words anywhere.

Negative: no face, no full person, no extra text, no gibberish letters, no watermark, no logo, no UI, no distorted shapes.
```
