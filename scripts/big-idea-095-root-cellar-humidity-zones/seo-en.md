## YouTube SEO Description (EN)

Root cellar storage runs on three zones, not one room. Match each crop to the right air, and learn how to store vegetables for winter: carrots, beets and cabbage in cold, wet air; onions and garlic in cold, dry air; potatoes in a damp middle.

You'll also see why apples don't belong beside potatoes, and three stand-ins when you have no dug cellar: a buried can, an unheated space, or a cool basement corner.
