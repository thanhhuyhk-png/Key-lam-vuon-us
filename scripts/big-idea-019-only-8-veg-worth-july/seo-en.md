The secret isn't your zone or your green thumb—it's matching days to maturity against your first frost date, then subtracting two weeks for the fall factor (when short fall days slow plant growth). Plant radishes and you've got food in 21–30 days. Grow spinach and kale that actually taste sweeter after a light frost. Squeeze in summer squash before the window closes. All eight vegetables finish before the frost hits. Peppers, melons, tomatoes from seed, and winter squash don't—the calendar runs out first, and there's no shame in skipping them.

Your one job today: find your average first frost date online, count backward, and you'll know exactly what still has time. Plant smart, skip what won't finish, and enjoy a harvest nobody expects.
