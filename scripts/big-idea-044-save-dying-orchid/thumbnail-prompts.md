# Thumbnail Prompts — Save a Dying Orchid in 5 Steps

**Mode:** MODE 1 — Concept Test (kênh đang Browse demotion)
**Concept-pair:** Before→After split (A, đã chốt GATE, sửa theo luật số) × Single-hero/timeline-badge (B)

---

## Biến thể A — Before→After (concept GATE, chữ đã trim ≤5 từ)

| Mục | Giá trị |
|---|---|
| Text | VÀNG `5 STEPS BACK` (3 từ) · TRẮNG `EVEN DRY ROOTS` (3 từ) |
| Layout | ① Before→After 60/40 (bên phải/hoa nổi bật hơn) |
| Cảm-xúc-đích (G4) | Hy vọng/chắc-thắng — hoa nở đối lập với rễ khô héo |

### MASTER PROMPT A

```
A photorealistic 16:9 YouTube thumbnail for a US home-gardening channel, audience 50-80. Vivid saturated colors, ultra high contrast, blurred windowsill background. NO human face, no full person (a single hand as prop is allowed only if described).

LAYOUT: a before-after split, NOT 50/50 — the right side dominates about 60% of the frame, bright and vivid; the left side is smaller, about 40%, dim and desaturated.
LEFT SIDE (~40%, dim): a limp Phalaenopsis orchid with drooping wrinkled leaves, roots visible through a clear pot, some roots silvery and shriveled, others brown and mushy. A red dashed circle around the worst roots and a red ❌.
RIGHT SIDE (~60%, bright, dominant): the same kind of orchid, plump white roots, a tall spike of white and purple flowers in full bloom. A green ✅ on this side.

TEXT — render EXACTLY two short lines in very thick bold sans-serif, heavy black outline + drop shadow, in the negative space, NOT covering the plants: line 1 "5 STEPS BACK" in bright YELLOW, line 2 "EVEN DRY ROOTS" in WHITE. The ONLY text in the entire image is exactly these two lines, spelled letter for letter, nothing else, no other letters, numbers, or words anywhere.

Negative: no face, no full person, no extra text, no gibberish letters, no watermark, no logo, no UI, no distorted shapes.
```

## Biến thể B — Single Hero + Timeline framing (concept khác hẳn A)

| Mục | Giá trị |
|---|---|
| Text | VÀNG `NO ROOTS LEFT?` (3 từ) · TRẮNG `IT CAN COME BACK` (4 từ) |
| Layout | ② Single hero — cận cảnh cây trơ trụi, không hoa, không rễ nhìn thấy |
| Cảm-xúc-đích (G4) | Cảm giác "gần như tuyệt vọng" khác hẳn A (A = hy vọng qua before→after; B = đồng cảm với người đang hoảng) |

### MASTER PROMPT B

```
A photorealistic 16:9 YouTube thumbnail for a US home-gardening channel, audience 50-80. Vivid saturated colors, ultra high contrast, blurred kitchen table background. NO human face, no full person (a single hand as prop is allowed only if described).

SCENE: ONE single hero object — a bare, leafless Phalaenopsis orchid stub with almost no roots, held gently in a single hand over a kitchen table, looking nearly hopeless. Offset to one side, leaving clean negative space for text.

TEXT — render EXACTLY two short lines in very thick bold sans-serif, heavy black outline + drop shadow, in the negative space, NOT covering the plant or hand: line 1 "NO ROOTS LEFT?" in bright YELLOW, line 2 "IT CAN COME BACK" in WHITE. The ONLY text in the entire image is exactly these two lines, spelled letter for letter, nothing else, no other letters, numbers, or words anywhere.

Negative: no face, no full person, no extra text, no gibberish letters, no watermark, no logo, no UI, no distorted shapes.
```
