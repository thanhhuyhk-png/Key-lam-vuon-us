## DESCRIPTION

Can you grow a pineapple from the top? Yes. This video shows how to grow a pineapple from a store-bought top, step by step, and what to expect from the first year. University extension guides put the wait from planting to harvest at about two years.

**In this video:**
- How to twist off and prepare a pineapple crown
- The detail at the base that can lead to rot, and how long to let it dry
- Pot size, planting depth, light and watering
- The temperature numbers that decide whether it survives winter
- An honest timeline: a green plant first, fruit much later

A pineapple top is easy to start, but it is a slow project. This video pulls together guidance from University of Florida, NC State and other garden sources, and it's upfront about where they differ.
