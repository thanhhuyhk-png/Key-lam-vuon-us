## DESCRIPTION

**How to Plant Garlic in a Pot — Grow a Full Bulb From One Clove**

Plant one garlic clove in a pot this fall, and next summer harvest a whole bulb of your own. This video shows you exactly how to grow garlic from cloves using just a pot, soil, and a bit of winter patience.

**In this video, you'll learn:**
- Which garlic clove to plant and why seed garlic beats grocery store bulbs
- Container gardening essentials: pot size, soil, spacing, and planting depth  
- How garlic uses winter cold to transform one clove into many — and what to do if you harvest a "round"
- Step-by-step harvest and curing, plus how to save the biggest bulbs for replanting next year
- A spring bonus: removing garlic scapes and using them in cooking

Garlic does well in containers, and the cold season does much of the work for you. The secret is fall planting: garlic needs about two months of temperatures between 32 and 50 degrees Fahrenheit for the cloves to form. That cold isn't just survival — it's the biological switch that turns one round bulb into separate cloves. Bring the pot indoors too early, and you'll harvest a solid ball instead. But here's the good news: even a "round" can be replanted the next season to become a full bulb. Next summer you'll be brushing dirt off homegrown garlic, and next fall you can save your biggest bulbs to plant again.

This method works where winters get properly cold — and once you grow your first crop, you'll have free seed garlic for years to come.
