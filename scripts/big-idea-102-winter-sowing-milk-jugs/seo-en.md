## YouTube SEO Description (EN — `en-US` ONLY)

Winter sowing in milk jugs lets you start sturdy seedlings outdoors through winter without grow lights, heat mats, or crowded indoor shelves (you'll just need potting mix, duct tape, and a weather-resistant marker). This guide covers how to prep a gallon milk jug properly, six cold-hardy seeds that thrive from December through February, three warm-season favorites to hold back until spring, and three popular crops where university extension guides actually disagree.

You'll also learn the coffee filter trick for drainage holes, why regular household markers fade by March, how to avoid the sunny spring heat trap, and how to separate crowded seedlings with the brownie method once they reach two sets of true leaves.
