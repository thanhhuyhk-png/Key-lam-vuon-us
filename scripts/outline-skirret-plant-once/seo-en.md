SAFETY NOTES - please read before you plant or eat anything:
- Rhubarb: eat the STALKS only, never the leaves. The leaves carry more oxalic acid than the stalks do.
- Jerusalem artichokes (sunchokes): these contain inulin, a starch that isn't common in most American diets. They may cause gas if you're not used to them. Start with a small serving.
- Eat only what you planted, in the bed where you planted it. This video is not a foraging guide.
