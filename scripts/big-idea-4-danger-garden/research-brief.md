### Câu 2: Rue Furanocoumarins — Science Verified ⚠️ YMYL

**Mechanism (PMC verified):**
- Rue (Ruta graveolens) contains furanocoumarins: **5-methoxypsoralen (bergapten)** and **8-methoxypsoralen (xanthotoxin)**
- Mechanism: Furanocoumarin + UV-A light (320–380nm) → DNA cross-linking in skin cells → phytophotodermatitis
- Result: erythema, fluid blisters, edema, hyperpigmentation
- **Severity documented:** Cases with **85% body surface area burns, second-degree severity**
- Tanning beds + rue contact = "alarming association" (separate PMC case study)

**Nguồn (peer-reviewed):**
- [PMC — Tanning Beds, Rue, and Major Burns (2022)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8830838/)
- [PubMed — Rue-associated phytophototoxicity](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17303046/)
- [PMC — Phytophotodermatitis Induced by Phototoxic Plants (2024)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11355232/)

**⚠️ YMYL Hedge cho script:**
- KHÔNG viết: "Rue will burn your skin" (overclaim)
- VIẾT: "Contact with rue followed by sun exposure has been documented to cause severe blistering reactions in some people — reactions that look and behave like second-degree burns. This is documented in peer-reviewed medical literature. If you have rue in your garden, wear gloves and long sleeves when handling it — especially on sunny days."
- KHÔNG suggest treatment, chỉ reference "seek medical attention" nếu reaction occurs

### Câu 3: Safe Substitutes — Verified Actionable

**Fargesia (clumping bamboo) — xác nhận hoạt động:**
- Zones: **5–9** (Fargesia rufa, Fargesia murielae) — widely available
- Spread rate: only **4–6 inches per year** (vs. running bamboo: feet per year)
- Height: **8–16 feet** — effective privacy screen
- Available at: major online nurseries, garden centers
- Nguồn: [NC State Extension — Fargesia robusta](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/fargesia-robusta/)

**Catmint (Nepeta) as rue substitute:**
- Same grey-green foliage aesthetic
- Confirmed non-toxic to children and pets (ASPCA safe list)
- Pest deterrent properties documented (deters aphids, Japanese beetles)
- [NEED DATA: specific peer-reviewed source on catmint pest deterrent — use "anecdotally reported" hedge]

**Liatris spicata (blazing star) as loosestrife substitute:**
- Native to eastern North America
- Confirmed high pollinator value — specialist bee species use it
- Available zones 3–8, widely sold
- Nguồn: [NC State Extension — Liatris spicata]

**American wisteria (Wisteria frutescens):**
- Native to southeastern US
- Confirmed "dramatically less aggressive" than Asian species
- Same flower color/fragrance profile
- Zones 5–9

### Câu 4: Legal Status — Specific Bans Verified

**Purple Loosestrife:**
- Banned or restricted in **30+ states**
- Listed as **Federal Noxious Weed** by USDA
- Specific bans confirmed: California, Minnesota, Illinois, Massachusetts, Connecticut, Vermont, Pennsylvania, Washington State
- Cannot legally be bought, sold, transported, or planted in these states
- Seed production: **2–3 million seeds per plant per year** (mature plant with ~30 flowering stems)
  - Range across sources: 2.5M–2.7M (some sources say "up to 2.7M")
  - Use: "up to 2 million seeds per plant" as conservative, defensible figure
- Nguồn: [Minnesota DNR — Purple Loosestrife](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/invasives/aquaticplants/purpleloosestrife/index.html), [Hunker — Illegal States](https://www.hunker.com/1819262/invasive-flowering-plant-purple-loosestrife-illegal-us-states/)

**Bishop's Weed (Goutweed):**
- Prohibited/restricted in: **Massachusetts, Vermont, Connecticut, Wisconsin**
- MA: illegal to purchase, plant, or transport
- VT: on Vermont Noxious Weed Watch List
- First invasive escape documented: Rhode Island, 1863
- Nguồn: [Vermont Invasives — Goutweed](https://www.vtinvasives.org/invasive/goutweed-or-bishops-weed)

### Câu 5: "3 Questions" Framework — Expert Basis

**Confirmed by Purdue University Extension:**
- "Before you buy, spend a few minutes researching possible problems a plant might bring to your garden"
- Specifically flags: rhizome spread (mint example given), toxic compounds, invasive behavior
- Nguồn: [Purdue Extension — Spreading Ornamental Plants: Virtues & Vices (PDF)](https://www.extension.purdue.edu/extmedia/HO/HO-295-W.pdf)

**MSU Extension confirms:**
- Check invasive plant lists before purchasing
- Research spreading mechanism (rhizome vs. stolon vs. seed)
- Nguồn: [MSU Extension — Invasive Species](https://www.canr.msu.edu/resources/understanding_and_managing_invasive_plant_species)

**Framework is defensible:** The 3 questions map directly to extension office guidance. Script can say: "University extension programs across the country agree: three things you should check before any plant goes in the ground."

## Lily of the Valley — Full YMYL Check ⚠️

- Contains **30+ cardiac glycosides** including convallatoxin, convallamarin, convallarin
- ASPCA: **Top 10 most dangerous garden plants** for dogs and cats
- Dose: **As few as 2 leaves** can be a fatal dose for young children or pets
- Vase water is also toxic — cardiac glycosides leach into water
- Emergency numbers for script:
  - Human: Poison Control **800-222-1222**
  - Pets: ASPCA Poison Control **(888) 426-4435**
- Nguồn: [ASPCA — Lily of the Valley Toxic Plants](https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/aspca-poison-control/toxic-and-non-toxic-plants/lily-valley), [WebMD — Lily of the Valley Poisoning](https://www.webmd.com/first-aid/lily-of-the-valley-poisoning)

**⚠️ YMYL Hedge cho script:**
- VIẾT: "The entire plant contains compounds that affect heart rhythm. According to the ASPCA, as few as two leaves can cause serious harm to children or pets. If you have young children or animals, this plant should not be in your garden."
- Luôn kèm: "If accidental ingestion occurs, call Poison Control immediately at 800-222-1222."
- KHÔNG overclaim "will kill" — dùng "can cause serious harm" / "documented fatalities in literature"
