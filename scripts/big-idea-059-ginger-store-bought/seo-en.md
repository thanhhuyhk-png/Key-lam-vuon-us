## DESCRIPTION

How to grow ginger from a store-bought root: pick the right one, soak it, pot it, and watch for green shoots in about a month (sometimes up to eight weeks).

**In this video:**
- Which grocery store ginger is worth planting, and why extension offices disagree
- Soaking, pot size, and how deep to plant
- The watering habit that can rot the root before it sprouts
- An honest timeline: shoots first, ginger to eat takes months

Growing ginger from a grocery store root takes one pot, fresh potting mix, and a little patience. Extension guides from Vermont, Illinois and California agree on the basics, so this video pulls them together in five simple steps. Shoots are not ginger for the dinner table. That part takes months, and you'll get the timeline straight.
