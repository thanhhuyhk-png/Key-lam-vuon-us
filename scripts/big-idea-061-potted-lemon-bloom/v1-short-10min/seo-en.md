## DESCRIPTION

Your lemon tree won't bloom indoors? Five real reasons explain why—light, feeding, temperature, age, and the move itself. This video walks through what extension offices actually say about each, and how to spot the mistake that can drop the buds you already have.

**In this video:**
- Why light is the first thing to check—and the exact hours extension guides recommend
- The feeding mistake that pushes leaves instead of flowers
- Why your tree wants the room you don't—and the night temperature that helps
- How age affects a lemon grown from seed vs. a grafted tree
- The move indoors: why slow matters, and when to time it

Extension sources point the same way on light and on moving a tree indoors gradually. They don't agree on bloom-booster fertilizer. This video lays out where they differ, and it's upfront about where the research is limited.
