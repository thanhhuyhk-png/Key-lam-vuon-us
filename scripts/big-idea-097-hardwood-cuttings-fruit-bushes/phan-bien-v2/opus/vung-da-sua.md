And this next part is for you if your winters are hard. Stay with me, because there's a workaround.

Plant five: figs. Figs are one of the easiest fruit crops to propagate. [C21] Take eight to ten inches of dormant wood in winter. [C20] Cut straight below a bud, and slant the top cut. [C20]

Plant six: pomegranates. They root readily from winter cuttings. Cut pieces about as thick as a pencil. [C26] Unlike grapes, they usually get a dip in rooting powder. [C26, C27]
