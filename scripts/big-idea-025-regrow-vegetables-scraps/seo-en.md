## Regrow 6 Vegetables From Scraps, No Soil | Gardening Without Bending

---

Gardening without bending — Grow fresh vegetables on your kitchen counter using scraps you'd normally throw away. No soil, no digging, no bending down at all. Just a jar of water and a sunny windowsill.

In this video, you'll learn:
- Which 6 vegetables regrow fastest from kitchen scraps — and which one is ready in under a week
- The exact step-by-step methods for each, pulled directly from university extension research
- Daily water-changing techniques to keep your scraps mold-free and safe to eat, backed by USDA food-safety guidelines

Here's the full breakdown. Green onions are your fastest win — new shoots show up in about 6 days and keep regrowing, cutting after cutting, for weeks. Celery and cabbage take 2–3 weeks to be ready for soil. Romaine, bok choy, and leeks give you one bonus harvest of fresh greens for salads and stir-fries. This video also covers what most regrow guides leave out: these aren't full replacements for store-bought vegetables. They're smaller, sometimes milder or slightly bitter, and perfect for supplementing your kitchen without extra grocery trips. Changing the water daily isn't just a gardening tip — it's a real food-safety measure. Bacteria in room-temperature water can double in as little as 20 minutes, which is why the USDA and extension offices are strict about it. For viewers over 60, taking these simple precautions matters even more — the CDC notes that older adults face higher risk from foodborne illness. One quick check (look, smell, feel for slime) takes 10 seconds and keeps you safe. Start with your green onion scraps tonight. By next week, you'll have fresh shoots ready to snip.
