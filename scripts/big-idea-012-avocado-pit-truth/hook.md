## Biến thể để chọn

**B — Question:**
> "You ate the avocado — now you're holding that big pit, wondering if you can grow it. You can. But should you expect avocados? The honest answer surprises almost everyone, and it starts with one number: out of a hundred pits, barely one ever makes good fruit."

**C — Contrast (before/after):**
> "Everyone plants an avocado pit dreaming of a tree full of fruit. Here's what actually happens: years of waiting, and almost never a single avocado. But there's a second thing that happens — one nobody talks about — and it might be the real reason to grow it."

**D — Story (micro-scenario, nostalgia):**
> "Back when, my grandmother kept an avocado pit in a juice glass on the kitchen sill — toothpicks and all. She never got an avocado off it. Not one. And yet she kept that plant for years. Today I'll tell you why she was right — and the five things nobody tells you about growing one."
> *(⚠️ Narrator integrity: "grandmother" = folklore/nostalgia, KHÔNG phải bằng chứng cá nhân — đúng luật kênh.)*

**E — Listicle-direct (bám title):**
> "There are five things nobody tells you about growing an avocado from a seed — and the first one might stop you from making a mistake half the people watching this have already made. Number one: that pit will almost never give you the avocado you just ate. Here's why that's actually good news."
