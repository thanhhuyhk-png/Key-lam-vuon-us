## YouTube SEO Description (EN)

Amish gardening without the myth: Amish farmers still plow, with horses. What's worth borrowing is one horse-era habit, putting manure back on the land, plus three fall steps from extension research that protect soil structure and cut down on weeds. No rototiller to buy or rent.

The four habits: snip spent plants at the soil line, spread manure in fall, cover beds with shredded leaves, and plant right through the mulch in spring. You'll also learn the one situation where tilling once still makes sense.

FOOD SAFETY: Never use pig, dog, or cat waste in a vegetable garden. Compost chicken manure before use. Raw manure needs 120 days on the soil before harvesting crops that touch the ground, and 90 days for other crops (USDA organic guideline, via Penn State Extension). Keep pets out of the vegetable garden and wash hands for 20 seconds after handling manure. Ask your county extension office if you're unsure.
