## Title
7 No-Bend Garden Setups That Save Your Back After 60

## Description
If gardening leaves your back aching for two days, the problem usually isn't your age — it's how your garden is set up. These seven simple, no-bend setups let you plant, weed, water, and harvest standing up, so you can keep doing what you love without the soreness the next morning.

Backed by guidance from university extension services and arthritis specialists, these are practical changes you can start this weekend — most for under fifty dollars. From standing-height beds to the one free fix almost everyone gets backwards, here's how to build a garden that works with your body, not against it.

🌱 Got a tip that saved your back? Share it in the comments — other gardeners read them.

⚠️ This video is for general information. If you have a back or joint condition, check with your doctor before changing your routine.
