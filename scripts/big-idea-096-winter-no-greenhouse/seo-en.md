## YouTube SEO Description (EN)

No greenhouse needed to keep a winter vegetable garden going. Match each crop to its cold limit, then add just enough cover: fabric, hoops and plastic, an old window as a cold frame, or straw over roots.

Hardy greens and carrots can take hard freezes after a cold autumn, while lettuce needs more help. And when days drop below about ten hours of light, most plants stop growing, so a winter garden is a living pantry rather than a new harvest.
