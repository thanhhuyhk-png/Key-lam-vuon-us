```
Build a raised garden bed for under $15: the exact cut list, tool list, and a free way to fill it, using cedar fence pickets from the hardware store.

In this video you'll learn:
- The full shopping list and cut list for a 4x3-foot raised garden bed using cheap cedar fence pickets
- Why the real "under $15" price depends on where you live — and what to do if your total runs closer to $20-25
- How to build the frame with simple tools: a saw, a drill, and pre-drilled corner posts
- A free, eco-friendly way to block weeds using plain cardboard instead of landscape fabric
- How to fill most of the bed for free using a layered method (lasagna gardening / simplified hugelkultur) instead of buying bags of soil

This build uses three untreated cedar fence pickets, one 2x2 furring strip for the corner posts, and a small box of exterior screws. Untreated wood matters here — you don't want chemicals from pressure-treated lumber near the soil you're growing food in.

Prices vary a lot by region, so the video is upfront about it: cedar pickets can run anywhere from $3 to $5+ each, which can push your total closer to $20-25 depending on your store. Even at that price, it's still a fraction of the $100+ raised bed kits sold online.

Once the frame is built, the video walks through siting it for 6-8 hours of daily sun, laying down plain cardboard to smother grass and weeds for free, and filling it in layers — bulky brown material on the bottom, nitrogen-rich green material in the middle, and compost or topsoil only on top, where the roots actually live.

Build it and drop your final cost in the comments — let's see who gets closest to $15.

Related build ideas: a compost bin, a vertical planter — vote in the comments for what to build next.
```
