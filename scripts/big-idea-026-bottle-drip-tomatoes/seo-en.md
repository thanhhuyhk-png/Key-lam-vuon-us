Building a drip irrigation system doesn't require fancy equipment or a big budget. These three 2-liter bottles cost nothing and sit on top of recycled plastic. The key is understanding one simple rule: keep the bottle cap at the soil surface, never buried underground. When the cap gets covered with dirt, the whole system fails within 24 hours — no air in, no water out. Get that right, and you'll have steady, even moisture reaching your tomato roots exactly when they need it most. University research from Penn State, Iowa State, and University of Maryland Extension confirms that consistent watering is the real secret to healthy tomatoes and a bigger harvest.
