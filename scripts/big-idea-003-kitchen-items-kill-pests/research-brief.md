## Cau hoi 1: Dish soap (insecticidal soap) diet aphid va mealybug nhu the nao — nong do pha chinh xac, co che, bao lau thi chet, nguong an toan cho cay?

**Findings:**

- Insecticidal soap giet aphid va mealybug bang 3 co che dong thoi: (1) pha vo mang te bao con trung gay mat nuoc nhanh, (2) lam sach lop sap bao ve (waxy cuticle) tren than con trung, (3) xam nhap vao lo tho (spiracles) lam giam oxy. (Clemson University Extension — HGIC, cap nhat gan day) — https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/insecticidal-soaps-for-garden-pest-control/

- Nong do pha chuan: **2.5 den 5 tablespoons tren 1 gallon nuoc** (tuong duong 1–2% dung dich). Nguong 3% co the gay chay la; mot so cay nhay cam co the bi ton thuong o nguong thap hon 1.5%. (Clemson University Extension — HGIC) — https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/insecticidal-soaps-for-garden-pest-control/

- Soap KHONG co residual effect — con trung phai bi uot truc tiep khi xit moi chet. Nen xit lai moi **4 den 7 ngay** de kiem soat sau nhu spider mite va scale. (Colorado State University Extension, cap nhat gan day) — https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/insect-control-insecticidal-soap/

- Phytotoxicity: **Khong xit khi nhiet do tren 90 degF** va khong xit duoi anh nang chinh trua — nhiet do cao lam soap kho nhanh, tang nguy co chay la. Neu chua thu, test tren mot phan nho truoc 1–2 ngay. (UConn Extension — Home Garden Education Office) — https://homegarden.cahnr.uconn.edu/factsheets/insecticidal-soaps/

- Cac loai cay nhay cam voi soap gom: Japanese maple, sweet pea, horse chestnut, mot so begonia, fern, poinsettia — tranh xit cac cay nay. (UConn Extension) — https://homegarden.cahnr.uconn.edu/factsheets/insecticidal-soaps/

- Hoat chat chinh trong insecticidal soap la **potassium salts of fatty acids** — khac hoan toan voi dish detergent (goc hoa dau tong hop). (Iowa State University Extension, 2007) — https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/faq/can-i-use-dish-soap-instead-insecticidal-soap-treat-insect-pests

## Cau hoi 2: Dish soap spray an toan cho tre em va thu cung toi muc nao — bang chung "khong luu ton du sau khi kho", khong doc voi ong/thien dia sau kho, va rui ro cua dish soap thuong vs insecticidal soap?

**Findings:**

- [YMYL] Insecticidal soap co **mammalian toxicity thap** — duoc mo ta la "virtually non-toxic to animals and birds" (Clemson HGIC). Tuy nhien, "co the gay kich ung nhe cho da hoac mat" (UConn Extension). Day la "contact-only" — KHONG co residual sau khi kho. (Clemson University Extension — HGIC) — https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/insecticidal-soaps-for-garden-pest-control/

- [YMYL] Sau khi spray kho, insecticidal soap "khong con hieu qua diet con trung" — do do KHONG doc voi ong va thien dia sau khi kho tren mat la. "Most beneficial insects are not harmed by soap sprays." Tuy nhien, **au trung mem than** (lacewing larvae, lady beetle larvae) co the bi anh huong khi xit truc tiep — mot nghien cuu ghi nhan 15% au trung bi diet tu 1 lan xit. (Iowa State University Extension, 2007) — https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/faq/can-i-use-dish-soap-instead-insecticidal-soap-treat-insect-pests

- [YMYL] Khac biet quan trong: **Dish soap / detergent KHONG phai insecticidal soap.** "Dry dish detergent va all clothes-washing detergents are far too harsh" — cac phu gia trong do co the gay doc cho cay (phytotoxic), pha huy lop sap bao ve tren la, va o nhiem nguon nuoc. Dish soap thuong la chat tay hoa dau tong hop (petrochemical detergent), KHONG phai potassium salts of fatty acids. (Clemson HGIC; Iowa State Extension) — https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/insecticidal-soaps-for-garden-pest-control/ | https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/faq/can-i-use-dish-soap-instead-insecticidal-soap-treat-insect-pests

- [YMYL] Insecticidal soap **doc hai voi ca va sinh vat duoi nuoc** — khong xit gan ao, kenh, suoi. (UConn Extension) — https://homegarden.cahnr.uconn.edu/factsheets/insecticidal-soaps/

- Nong do dish soap qua cao (>3%) co the "dot chay la cay, dac biet khi cay bi stress, nhiet do >90 degF va do am cao". (Clemson HGIC) — https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/insecticidal-soaps-for-garden-pest-control/

## Cau hoi 3: Baking soda trong vuon — bang chung hoat dong nhu thuoc TRI NAM (powdery mildew), KHONG diet aphid; cong thuc cu the, va gioi han?

**Findings:**

- Baking soda (sodium bicarbonate) la thuoc **tri nam** (fungicide), khong phai thuoc diet con trung — khong co bang chung nao cho thay no giet hoac duoi aphid, mealybug, hay bat ky loai sau nao. (Colorado State University Extension, cap nhat gan day) — https://extension.colostate.edu/topic-areas/yard-garden/powdery-mildews-2-902/

- Cong thuc duoc dan tu nghien cuu Cornell University (tren hoa hong): **1 tablespoon baking soda + 2.5 tablespoons Sunspray oil (dau horticultural nhe) trong 1 gallon nuoc**. Mot cong thuc don gian hon: **1 teaspoon baking soda trong 1 quart nuoc** (tu cac nguon extension). (Colorado State University Extension) — https://extension.colostate.edu/topic-areas/yard-garden/powdery-mildews-2-902/

- Co che: Baking soda nang pH tren be mat la, tao moi truong kiem ma nam powdery mildew (Erysiphe spp.) khong the phat trien.

- Gioi han quan trong: Nguon CSU Extension ghi ro day **"van con la thi nghiem"** — "Use it at your own risk." Baking soda hieu qua hon de **ngan ngua** (prevention) hon la **dieu tri nang** (heavy active infection). (Colorado State University Extension) — https://extension.colostate.edu/topic-areas/yard-garden/powdery-mildews-2-902/

- Khi dung sai lieu luong, baking soda co the gay doc cho la va dat (soil toxicity). (Multi-source extension consensus)

- [NEED DATA: Khong tim duoc nghien cuu peer-reviewed Tier 1-2 kiem nghiem truc tiep baking soda vs aphid de xac nhan 100% "zero insecticidal effect" — tuy nhien khong co nguon nao cua extension cho rang baking soda co tac dung diet sau. Claim nay la "absence of evidence" chu chua phai "evidence of absence" qua thu nghiem kiem soat.]

## Cau hoi 4: Vinegar (acetic acid) trong vuon — bang chung la herbicide tiep xuc KHONG chon loc, nong do household (5%) vs horticultural (20-30%), va nguong nong do gay bong da/mat nguoi?

**Findings:**

- Vinegar la **herbicide tiep xuc khong chon loc** — "Will kill or damage any plants they touch." No pha vo mang te bao la, gay kho va chet mu nhanh khi tiep xuc, nhung **KHONG di chuyen xuong re** — do do cay co re sau (vd. dandelion) se **moc lai**. (University of Maryland Extension) — https://extension.umd.edu/resource/vinegar-alternative-glyphosate

- Nong do: **Giam bep thuong = 5% acetic acid.** Vinegar horticultural = **10–30% acetic acid.** Giam bep 5% chi hieu qua voi **cay con rat nho** (cotyledon stage, 2–4 la); cay lon hon se song sot. (University of Maryland Extension; NPIC — Oregon State University) — https://extension.umd.edu/resource/vinegar-alternative-glyphosate | https://npic.orst.edu/faq/vinegar.html

- [YMYL] **Nguong gay bong: Nong do acetic acid >= 11%** gay bong da va ton thuong mat nghiem trong, ke ca **mu loa (blindness)**. Tat ca san pham herbicide acetic acid dang ky EPA deu mang tu canh bao "DANGER". (University of Maryland Extension; NPIC — Oregon State University) — https://extension.umd.edu/resource/vinegar-alternative-glyphosate | https://npic.orst.edu/faq/vinegar.html

- [YMYL] Khi bay hoi o nhiet do cao, acetic acid co the gay kich ung duong ho hap khi hit phai. (NPIC — Oregon State University, National Pesticide Information Center) — https://npic.orst.edu/faq/vinegar.html

- Vinegar KHONG phai thuoc diet sau — khong co co che tac dung len con trung theo bat ky tham chieu extension nao. No la herbicide, khong phai insecticide. Dung len cay trong se giet cay. (University of Illinois Extension, 2020) — https://extension.illinois.edu/blogs/good-growing/2020-05-22-home-horticultural-remedies

- Hieu qua diet co khong tot bang glyphosate: Nghien cuu ghi nhan acetic acid chi tieu diet 28–45% co cobalt (crabgrass) ngay ca o nong do toi uu, trong khi can nhieu lan xit hon, tang chi phi lao dong. (University of Maryland Extension) — https://extension.umd.edu/resource/vinegar-alternative-glyphosate

## Cau hoi 5: Cinnamon va coffee grounds — bang chung thi nghiem extension cho thay KHONG diet/duoi sau hieu qua; va cong dung THAT cua chung la gi?

**Findings:**

**Cinnamon:**

- Thi nghiem tai **Texas A&M AgriLife Extension** (fireant.tamu.edu): 1 tablespoon bot que (ground cinnamon) rai tren to kien lua (fire ant mound), quan sat trong 1 thang — **THAT BAI, phan tac dung**. To kien duoc xu ly cho thay **hoat dong NHIEU HON** so voi to doi chung khong xu ly. (Texas A&M AgriLife Extension) — https://fireant.tamu.edu/entomologist-testing-puts-bite-on-many-home-fire-ant-treatments/

- Nghien cuu in vitro (petri dish) cho thay cinnamon co kha nang chong nam, nhung: cac thu nghiem nay KHONG co dat va cay con, va **khong co nghien cuu nao thu xit bot kho tren be mat dat** (cach dung pho bien cua nguoi lam vuon). Chua co bang chung khoa hoc kiem soat rang bot que kho dung tren dat co tac dung diet nam khi trong cay con. (GardenMyths.com tong hop nghien cuu — Tier 3 source; khong phai extension nhung phan anh dong thuan khoa hoc) — https://www.gardenmyths.com/cinnamon-stop-damping-off-seedlings/

- Cong dung that co tiem nang (nhung con la thi nghiem): Cinnamon essential oil va extract alcohol co kha nang chong nam trong dieu kien phong thi nghiem — co the co ung dung chong damping-off, nhung chua co confirmation o muc field trial co kiem soat. (NC State Extension — KHONG de cap den cinnamon trong tai lieu damping-off chinh thuc) — https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/damping-off-in-flower-and-vegetable-seedlings

**Coffee Grounds:**

- **Washington State University Extension** khang dinh ro rang: **"There is no published evidence that coffee grounds will repel or kill any garden pests."** (WSU Extension Publications) — https://pubs.extension.wsu.edu/product/using-coffee-grounds-in-gardens-and-landscapes-home-garden-series/

- Thi nghiem tai Texas A&M AgriLife Extension: 1 cup bã ca phe (tu chuong trinh Starbucks) rai tren to kien lua, theo doi 30 ngay — **THAT BAI.** "The home remedy of spreading one cup of used coffee grounds over a fire ant mound failed to kill the fire ants." (Texas A&M AgriLife Extension) — https://fireant.tamu.edu/entomologist-testing-puts-bite-on-many-home-fire-ant-treatments/

- Oregon State University Extension (Linda Brewer, certified soil scientist): Rai ba ca phe xung quanh cay khong giu oc sen di vi "there isn't enough caffeine left in coffee grounds to repel or kill slugs." (Oregon State University Extension) — https://extension.oregonstate.edu/news/coffee-grounds-boost-soil-health-help-control-slugs

- **Cong dung THAT cua coffee grounds** (duoc extension xac nhan): (1) **Soil amendment** — can bang cau truc dat, tang thoat nuoc khi duoc composting dung cach; (2) **Dinh duong** — ham luong nito >10% theo trong luong, nhung chi giai phong cham khi phan huy; (3) Co the **bo sung composting** toi da 20% theo the tich. (WSU Extension) — https://pubs.extension.wsu.edu/product/using-coffee-grounds-in-gardens-and-landscapes-home-garden-series/

- [YMYL — canh bao nguoi dung] Ba ca phe **TUOI (fresh grounds) doc hai voi cay** — lam giam nuong hat va tang truong cay con. Khong bao gio rai day qua 1/2 inch truc tiep len dat vi cau truc min se gay nen dat, chan am va khi. (WSU Extension) — https://pubs.extension.wsu.edu/product/using-coffee-grounds-in-gardens-and-landscapes-home-garden-series/

## YMYL Flags — Claims Can Hedge Trong Script

1. **Insecticidal soap + pets/kids:** "Virtually non-toxic" la dac tinh cua insecticidal soap thuong mai — KHONG ap dung cho dish soap thuong (chua phu gia). Hedge: "Insecticidal soap is considered low-toxicity once dry — but keep kids and pets away until the spray has fully dried, and always use a product labeled for garden use, not kitchen dish soap."

2. **Dish soap vs insecticidal soap distinction:** Dish soap co chua surfactant hoa dau tong hop co the gay doc cho dat, con trung co ich, va lam chay la cay. Khong khuyen nghi dung dish soap thuong nhu thay the.

3. **Vinegar nong do cao (>=11%):** Gay bong da nghiem trong va mu loa. Neu viewer co o nha horticultural vinegar (ngang 20–30%), PHAI mac gang tay va kinh bao ve. Giam bep 5% an toan hon nhung khong hieu qua tren cay lon.

4. **Fresh coffee grounds doc hai voi cay con:** Bã ca phe tuoi giam nuong hat — khong bao gio rai ngay quanh goc cay con hoac hon hop dau.

5. **Insecticidal soap doc voi ca va sinh vat duoi nuoc:** Khong xit gan ao hoac kenh thoat nuoc.

## NEED DATA

- **Cinnamon vs damping-off (field trial):** Khong tim duoc RCT hoac field trial co kiem soat tu nguon Tier 1-2 xac nhan hieu qua cua bot que kho rai tren be mat dat. NC State Extension va MSU Extension deu khong de cap den cinnamon trong tai lieu chinh thuc ve damping-off. Chi co in vitro data (petri dish) — KHONG du manh de claim hieu qua thuc te trong vuon.

- **Thoi gian chiet xuat (time-to-kill) chinh xac cho soap vs aphid:** Cac nguon extension mo ta co che nhung khong cho so gio/phut cu the de aphid chet sau khi xit. Nguon Tier 1 chi ghi "contact kill" — "insects must be wetted by spray to be killed."

- **So sanh dinh luong dish soap thuong vs insecticidal soap ve phytotoxicity:** Co consensus rang dish soap gay hai hon, nhung khong co nguon Tier 1 cung cap thi nghiem doi chung so sanh truc tiep.
