## Research Notes (key claims + sources)
- Insecticidal/dish soap kills soft-bodied aphids, mealybugs, whiteflies, mites by disrupting cell membranes + removing protective wax; contact-only, no residual → not toxic to pollinators/pets once dry. 1–2% solution (1–2 tsp/pint). Test for phytotoxicity; avoid full sun / >90°F. [Clemson HGIC, UConn, CSU, UMN]
- Dish soap may contain degreasers/additives that can injure plants → recommend cautious use/testing. [UMN Extension]
- Baking soda = fungicide (raises leaf pH vs powdery mildew/fungal disease), prevention-focused; NOT documented for aphid control. [UF/IFAS]
- Vinegar (acetic acid) = non-selective contact herbicide, ruptures plant cells, burns any plant touched; 5% household; 20–30% horticultural burns human skin/eyes. [MSU, OSU, UMD, U Illinois]
- Cinnamon: extension testing showed MORE ant activity vs control; UC IPM says won't relieve fungus gnats. Real use = anti-fungal for seedlings. [TAMU fireant, UC IPM]
- Coffee grounds: AgriLife test — 1 cup over fire ant mound did NOT kill; repellent claims mixed/weak. Real use = compost/soil amendment, mild barrier. [TAMU AgriLife]
