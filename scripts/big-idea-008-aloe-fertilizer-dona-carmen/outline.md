### PART 3 — THE 5-STEP RECIPE (senior-accessible, no blender) (7:00–13:00)
Mỗi bước: what → how (no heavy tools) → why → quick proof.
1. **Harvest the right leaf** — outer, thick, mature leaf; cut at base with kitchen knife (no bending if plant is at waist height — link to setup video #009).
2. **Bucket-steep (NO blender)** — slice leaf, drop in 1 gallon of water, let sit 24 hours. "No blender to lift, no mess to clean." (Accessibility Anchor)
3. **Dilute & soil-drench** — 1 part aloe water to 10 parts water; pour around base every 2 weeks, morning. Lightweight watering can / spray bottle.
4. **Pest barrier (optional)** — add a few drops of neem oil, shake, spray leaves showing soft-bodied pests. HEDGE as supplement.
5. **Rooting dip (the "neighbors will ask" payoff)** — dip cuttings/seedling roots in diluted aloe gel 20 min before planting → reduces transplant shock. [SOURCE: rooting-hormone studies]
